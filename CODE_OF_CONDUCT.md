# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free, and inclusive project. Participation is
welcome regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and respect security-reporting boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disengage when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate
disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and other conduct inappropriate in
a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation for behavior that violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider
context, impact, and whether harmful behavior continues after a warning.

Use GitHub's content-reporting tools for abusive content. For a project-specific concern,
contact the maintainer through the contact information on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Reederey87). Do not publish sensitive personal
details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as the available
channel permits.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
